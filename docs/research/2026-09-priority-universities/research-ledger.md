# Priority university and campus research — batch 01

Access date for every linked source: **2026-09-26**. This is a source-backed **draft preparation**, not an institutional verification action. No administrator has reviewed these facts inside DAE2UNI. The import file uses `strategy: "skip"`, contains no programs, and cannot set publication, HEC-recognition, or source-verification status. The importer creates new records as `draft`, `pending_review`, and HEC-`unverified` even where an official source discusses recognition. Recheck live sources before any future apply action.

## Contract and database assessment

The current JSON import contract permits university `slug`, `name`, `abbreviation`, `sector`, `institutionType`, `provinceOrTerritory`, `charterAuthority`, optional `hecProfileUrl`, `source.officialUrl`, campuses, and programs. It **does not accept** established year, recognition bodies, contact details, admissions URL, `recordStatus`, `hecRecognitionStatus`, source verification fields, or a manual verification timestamp. Those fields are deliberately absent from `bulk-import.json`; relevant findings below are research notes only. Campus `isActive` defaults to true, but inclusion means only that an official current page establishes the campus; it is not a claim about admissions or HEC campus approval.

Read-only local catalogue inspection found **1 university, 0 programs, 0 verification-history records**. The only university is the existing `uet-lahore` draft, with a `www.uet.edu.pk` source domain and five campus names. Normalized name/slug/domain/campus comparison therefore excludes UET Lahore. No matching normalized names, slugs, website/source domains, or campus identities were found for the nine import candidates among the other local records (there are no others). `skip` is still required because the database can change after this review.

Batch summary after the campus-level evidence audit: **11 institutions researched; 9 import-ready university drafts; 1 duplicate-review university; 1 unselected alternative.** The nine proposed universities now contain **29 in-scope campus proposals**, zero programs. Two of the original 31 campuses were removed: UET Taxila Chakwal and IUB Ahmedpur East. “Import-ready” means schema-ready for *preview*, not approval to apply or publish. [Machine-readable exclusions](./excluded-records.json) cover the two omitted universities and the two removed campuses.
